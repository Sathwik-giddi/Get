# Design review 009

Date: 2026-10-03
Scope: the decision view only, read as a first-time judge would read it: top to bottom,
one pass, no scrolling back up. The landing, the rail motif, and the officer panel are not
re-litigated.
Mode: audit after. Nothing below has been fixed yet.

## Findings

### 1. Two rails say the same thing — C-3 — HIGH

The main column runs a plan rail (steps 1-5: analyze_document three times, analyze_image,
make_decision) and then a reasoning rail (steps 1-3: analyze_document, analyze_image,
make_decision). Eight numbered items, three skills, twice. A reader who finishes step 5
and meets step 1 again concludes the page is repeating itself, because it is. The reasoning
chain already cites its skill per step, and the evidence column already attributes every
finding to its source, so the plan rail carries no information the page does not have
elsewhere. Remove it from the main view. Keep one summary line (how many skill calls ran on
how many sources, decided by which step) and keep the full plan in the raw trace where the
auditor, not the officer, finds it. The rail motif survives; it just runs once.

### 2. Confidence is squeezed into a gutter — R-31 — MEDIUM

93%, the bar, the rationale paragraph, and the run metadata share one narrow column beside
a 42px call. The rationale wraps to five thin lines and the run id line lands between the
number and its reason, breaking the one relationship that matters. Fold all of it into a
single strip under the call: the figure and the bar on the left, the rationale beside it,
the run metadata as one muted line underneath. Confidence reads as one thought instead of
four fragments.

### 3. The right column never ends — C-3, R-05 — MEDIUM

Evidence, risks, rejected alternatives, escalation trigger, all at identical weight, all
fully expanded, all the way down. Everything on this page cannot matter equally or nothing
does. Evidence and risks stay open: they are the proof and the doubt, the two things a
decision is made of. "Rejected first" folds into a native collapsible section. It is real
content one click away, not hidden content: native disclosure is keyboard-operable and
announced. Escalation stays visible; it is two lines and it is the handoff.

### 4. The planner narrates, it does not plan — R-36 — LOW

"A structured document carries the authoritative forecast and constraints." "A free-text
source carries field observations not in any document." Any three documents in any order
would get these sentences. The `why` lines are interchangeable, which is why the three
analyze_document rows look copy-pasted. Name the actual source and its actual job per row:
the bulletin constrains, the dispatch note commits the window, the field report observes.
Written against the manifest, not the template.

## Not findings

- The officer panel placement is correct where it is. It closes the page after the
  handoff, which is the last thing before raw data.
- The raw trace stays expanded-collapsible as is. The auditor's view is not the clutter
  complaint.
- No new colors, no new type, no new components. This pass removes and folds; it adds
  nothing visible except a disclosure widget the platform provides.
