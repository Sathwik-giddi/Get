WAREHOUSE INCIDENT REPORT - BLOCK C, CENTRAL STORE
Reported by: K. Iyer, Store Supervisor, 09:40

Water entered Block C from the roof vent at about 04:10 during heavy rain. We found
standing water about 6 cm deep across roughly 60 percent of the floor when staff
arrived. Cold-chain pallets nearest the vent are affected.

We have moved 22 pallets to the dry bay. 18 pallets are still in place. The generator
room is not affected but the main supply has tripped on leakage detection.

The vaccine stock in the 18 exposed pallets is ours to save. The health department
closed office at 17:00 and their officer could not be reached on the mobile.
